How it works

  1. The host opens a room and shares the code.
  2. Players join from their own tab or device and pick a nickname.
  3. Each question has a timer. A correct answer scores 500 to 1000 points, more for speed, plus a small streak bonus.
  4. After every question the host sees how the room answered and who is ahead.
  5. The top three stand on a 3D podium at the end.

Two ways to connect

This browser: players join from other tabs or windows of the same browser. No server needed, ideal for a quick demo.

Network: a small Node relay carries messages between devices. Players on other devices must be able to reach the API's address.

Technical approach

  • Angular with signals. The host's browser is authoritative: it holds the answers, the timer and the scores.
  • Correct answers are only sent to players after the question closes.
  • Three.js builds the podium from basic shapes. Confetti respects reduced motion.
  • The optional Node API relays rooms over Server-Sent Events and can generate questions through a provider adapter: mock by default, anthropic with a key.

Limitations

Rooms live in memory and vanish on refresh. A host who closes the tab ends the game. There is no cheating protection beyond hiding answers until reveal. Generated questions must be checked by a person before use. Sample questions are original and written for this demo.
